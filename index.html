About Maa Chamunda Devi

|| Jai Maa Chamunda Devi ||

Maa Chamunda Devi Temple is one of the most revered Shakti Peeths located in the Kangra district of Himachal Pradesh. The temple is dedicated to Maa Chamunda, a fierce form of Goddess Durga, who symbolizes the power to destroy evil and protect her devotees.

According to ancient legends, this temple is believed to be the place where Goddess Durga killed the demons Chanda and Munda. The idol of the Goddess is considered highly divine and is adorned with a beautiful silver face and intricate jewelry.

Situated on the banks of the River Baner with the majestic Dhauladhar mountain range in the backdrop, the temple is a spiritual and scenic destination. It offers a serene environment for meditation and seeking blessings.

Thousands of devotees visit this holy shrine every year, especially during the festival of Navratri, to seek the blessings of Maa Chamunda for strength, protection, and prosperity.

How to Reach

By Road

Maa Chamunda Devi Temple is well-connected by road. It is approx 15 km from Dharamshala, 16 km from Kangra, and 10 km from Palampur. You can easily hire a taxi or take a local HRTC bus from these cities to reach the temple directly.

By Train

The nearest broad-gauge railway station is Pathankot (approx 85 km). From Pathankot, you can take the scenic Kangra Valley narrow-gauge toy train to Kangra station. From Kangra, taxis and buses are readily available for the temple.

By Air

The nearest airport is Gaggal Airport (Kangra Airport), which is approx 20 km away from the temple. It has regular flights connecting to Delhi and other major cities. From the airport, you can hire a taxi to reach the shrine.
